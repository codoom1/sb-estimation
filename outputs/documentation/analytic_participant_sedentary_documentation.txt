Dataset documentation: analytic_participant_sedentary.csv

Generated from reports/source/manuscript_main.Rmd using the 1h epoch analysis.
Final analytic sample size: 14,534 participants.
Selected sleep/nonwear cutoff: 90.9%.

Purpose
This participant-level dataset is intended to support future epidemiologic research using NHANES 2011-2014 wrist accelerometer-derived waking sedentary time. Each row represents one NHANES participant. The dataset summarizes valid daily accelerometer records to a participant-level estimate of usual daily waking sedentary time after excluding epochs classified as sleep/nonwear.

Source data and processing overview
The source accelerometer summaries combine CHAP wrist posture predictions with SWaN sleep/nonwear classifications. Epoch-level sitting percentages are converted to sedentary hours by multiplying percent sitting by epoch duration. Epochs with sleep/nonwear percentage greater than or equal to the selected cutoff are excluded from waking sedentary-time estimation. Daily totals are calculated first, then valid days are averaged within participant.

Unit of analysis
The analytic unit is the NHANES participant. The repeated epoch/day records are used to construct the participant-level sedentary, sleep/nonwear, and waking-time summaries, but they are not retained as separate analytic rows in this file.

Uncertainty columns
The SD, SE, and CI columns describe within-participant day-to-day variability across valid wear days. They are not NHANES population-level standard errors. Population-level uncertainty should be estimated with the NHANES survey design variables included in this file.

For sedentary time:
  sd_sed_hours = standard deviation of participant-day sedentary hours across valid days.
  se_sed_hours = sd_sed_hours / sqrt(n_days).
  sed_ci_l and sed_ci_u = mean_sed_hours +/- t_(0.975, n_days - 1) * se_sed_hours.

For sleep/nonwear time:
  sd_sleep_hours = standard deviation of participant-day sleep/nonwear hours across valid days.
  se_sleep_hours = sd_sleep_hours / sqrt(n_days).
  sleep_ci_l and sleep_ci_u = mean_sleep_hours +/- t_(0.975, n_days - 1) * se_sleep_hours.

For waking time:
  mean_wake_hours = 24 - mean_sleep_hours.
  se_wake_hours = se_sleep_hours.
  wake_ci_l = 24 - sleep_ci_u.
  wake_ci_u = 24 - sleep_ci_l.

Participants with one valid day
When n_days = 1, within-participant SD, SE, and CI cannot be estimated. These uncertainty columns are therefore left missing for one-day participants.

Survey design
Exam_weight_recal is the recalibrated 2011-2014 NHANES examination weight, created by dividing the original 2-year examination weight by two because the analysis combines the 2011-2012 and 2013-2014 cycles. PSU and Stratum are the masked NHANES variance-estimation variables. Analyses intended to produce nationally representative estimates should use Exam_weight_recal, PSU, and Stratum in an appropriate survey design object.

Variable dictionary
ID: NHANES participant identifier.
cutoff: selected sleep/nonwear percentage cutoff used to define waking epochs.
n_days: number of valid participant-days contributing to the participant-level mean.
mean_sed_hours: participant mean waking sedentary time in hours/day.
sd_sed_hours: within-participant standard deviation of daily waking sedentary hours.
se_sed_hours: within-participant standard error of mean_sed_hours.
sed_ci_l: lower 95% within-participant CI for mean_sed_hours.
sed_ci_u: upper 95% within-participant CI for mean_sed_hours.
mean_sleep_hours: participant mean sleep/nonwear time in hours/day.
sd_sleep_hours: within-participant standard deviation of daily sleep/nonwear hours.
se_sleep_hours: within-participant standard error of mean_sleep_hours.
sleep_ci_l: lower 95% within-participant CI for mean_sleep_hours.
sleep_ci_u: upper 95% within-participant CI for mean_sleep_hours.
mean_wake_hours: participant mean waking time in hours/day.
se_wake_hours: within-participant standard error of mean_wake_hours.
wake_ci_l: lower 95% within-participant CI for mean_wake_hours.
wake_ci_u: upper 95% within-participant CI for mean_wake_hours.
Age_group: age category used for subgroup analyses.
BMI_cat: BMI category derived from measured weight and height.
Race: NHANES race/ethnicity category available in the analysis data.
Gender: participant gender category available in the analysis data.
Education: coarse education category available in the analysis data.
Marital_status: marital status category available in the analysis data.
Exam_weight_recal: recalibrated 2011-2014 NHANES examination weight.
PSU: masked NHANES primary sampling unit.
Stratum: masked NHANES variance stratum.
Dataset: NHANES cycle, either 2011-2012 or 2013-2014.

Recommended use
Use this dataset for participant-level epidemiologic analyses of waking sedentary time. For descriptive national estimates and subgroup analyses, use the NHANES survey design variables. Do not treat rows as unweighted independent population observations when making nationally representative claims.
